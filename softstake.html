SHIOKAMBING – Soft Stake NFT

SHIOKAMBING Soft Stake introduces a different approach to NFT staking. Instead of always requiring an NFT to be transferred into a staking contract, a soft staking system may use NFT ownership in a connected wallet as the basis for rewards, points, or other benefits.

What Is Soft Stake?

Soft staking is a mechanism that may allow NFT holders to receive benefits while keeping their NFTs in their own wallets. The system can verify NFT ownership and determine eligibility according to predefined project rules.

This approach differs from traditional staking, where an NFT is commonly deposited or locked through a smart contract. However, soft staking is not a universal blockchain standard, so its implementation can differ from one project to another.

Soft Stake vs Traditional NFT Staking

Feature Soft Stake Traditional Stake
NFT Location May remain in the wallet Often deposited into a staking mechanism
Eligibility May be based on NFT ownership Usually based on staked assets
NFT Transfer Not always required Commonly required
Rewards Depends on the project Depends on the project

Potential Benefits of Soft Staking

The main appeal of soft staking is that eligible NFTs may remain under the user's wallet while the ownership is used to determine participation in a reward system.

  • Keep eligible NFTs in a personal wallet.
  • Participate in ownership-based reward programs.
  • Potentially avoid transferring NFTs to a staking contract.
  • Maintain direct visibility of NFT ownership on-chain.

The actual benefits depend entirely on the implementation and rules of the project.

Pay Attention to Wallet Permissions

Keeping an NFT in a wallet does not automatically mean every interaction is risk-free. A platform may still request a signature, transaction, or NFT approval.

Always review the wallet request before signing or approving a blockchain transaction.

Users should verify the smart contract address and confirm that the application is an official source before granting permissions.

Is Soft Stake Safe?

The safety of a soft staking system depends on the application, smart contract, wallet interaction, and overall project implementation. No blockchain mechanism should be considered completely risk-free.

Users should also understand that reward tokens or points may fluctuate in value and that project rules can change over time.

SHIOKAMBING Soft Stake highlights an alternative approach to NFT-based rewards through ownership verification. Understanding wallet permissions, smart contracts, and the specific reward rules can help users interact with soft staking systems more responsibly.
